1. Arriving
   • What are you feeling right now? Find a word for it, or a few. What might be behind it?
   • What's spinning in your mind? What do you need to put down before sleep?
2. The Day
   • What went well today? Where were you present, patient or kind? Let yourself be glad of it.
   • What's the moment from today you'd tell as a story, even a small one?
   • What gave you energy, and what drained it?
   • Who showed up for you, and who did you show up for?
3. One Moment, Looked at Again
   • Pick one moment that caused more harm than it needed to. How present were you in it? What came of it, for you and for anyone else?
   • Regret what deserves it, and leave out the guilt and the self-blame. What would you do differently, and is there anything to mend tomorrow?
4. The Best Part of You
   • What would the best part of yourself say about how you did today?
5. Closing
   • What are you grateful for from today?
   • You have lived this day. What do you want to carry into sleep?
