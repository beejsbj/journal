Ten structured sessions in four movements: 1–4 diagnosis (where you are and why you're stuck), 5–7 understanding (how the psyche works, what blocks), 8–9 transformation (building the new architecture), 10 integration (a full one-day protocol).

Answer each phase in your own words, as one message or several; the next phase comes a couple of minutes after you go quiet, or at once with /next. /skip, /back and /break are always there. One session every few days is the pace that holds.
