## Phase 1: How you'd be remembered
**1.1** When you're gone, what would you want people to say about you? Not what you achieved: what kind of person you were.
**1.2** What kind of person do you most want to be?
### Transition
Now two quick lists, without stopping to judge them.

## Phase 2: Two lists
**2.1** Desired: what do you want for yourself? Money, comfort, recognition, whatever is honestly on the list.
**2.2** Admired: what qualities do you most admire in other people? Think of the people you look up to, and what it is about them.
### Transition
Put the two lists side by side.

## Phase 3: Where they differ
**3.1** Where do the lists differ, and why do you think that is?
**3.2** Which quality you admire would you most like to grow in yourself?

## Phase 4: This week
**4.1** Take that quality. What would it look like in one ordinary situation this week that you can already name?
