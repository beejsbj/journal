## Phase 1: Comfort or growth
### Introduction
Five questions about living with limited time, one at a time. An answer doesn't have to come; living with the question is the point. Write what you have, then /next.
### Questions
**1.1** Where in your life or work are you choosing what's comfortable, when what's needed is a little discomfort?
**1.2** Take one choice in front of you. Does it make you smaller, or larger?
### Transition
Next, the standards you hold yourself to.

## Phase 2: Impossible standards
**2.1** What standards are you holding yourself to, at work or anywhere, that no one could actually meet?
**2.2** If you accepted that you'll never get on top of everything, what would you do differently today?
### Transition
Next, being who you are.

## Phase 3: Being who you are
**3.1** Where are you still measuring yourself against the person you think you ought to be, instead of the one you are?
**3.2** If no one were keeping score, how would you like to spend your time?
### Transition
Next, what you're holding back on.

## Phase 4: Holding back
**4.1** Where are you waiting until you feel you know what you're doing before you start?
**4.2** Almost everyone is improvising. What would you begin this week if you stopped waiting to feel ready?
### Transition
The last one is the widest.

## Phase 5: Work you won't see finished
**5.1** How would you spend your days if you cared less about seeing your efforts pay off in your lifetime?
**5.2** What's the one next thing that most needs doing? Only that one.
