## Phase 1: What happened
### Introduction
One moment that stung, looked at slowly. Take a recent one, still fresh.
### Questions
**1.1** What happened? Where were you, who was there, what was said or done? Just the facts, as a camera would have caught them.
**1.2** What did you feel? Name each feeling and give it a number from 0 to 100 for how strong it was.
### Transition
Now the thoughts that came with it.

## Phase 2: The thought
**2.1** What went through your mind? Write the thoughts as they came, in their own words, even the ones that sound unfair or extreme.
**2.2** Which one carries the most charge? Write it again on its own. That's the one to look at.
### Transition
Treat that thought as a claim, not a fact, and look at it the way a fair friend would.

## Phase 3: The evidence
**3.1** What supports the thought? Only things that happened, not how you read them.
**3.2** What doesn't fit it? Times it wasn't true, what you'd point out to a friend who thought this about themselves, anything you're leaving out.
### Transition
Now put the two side by side.

## Phase 4: A fairer view
**4.1** With all of it in view, what's a fairer way to put it? It doesn't have to be cheerful, only true to the evidence.
**4.2** Read it back. How strong is each feeling now, 0 to 100? Is there one thing you'll do next?
