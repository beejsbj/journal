## Phase 1: Empty it out
### Introduction
Everything that has your attention, out of your head and onto the page: big or small, work or home, thought through or not. Don't sort yet.
### Questions
**1.1** What's on your mind? List it all, one thing to a line.
### Transition
Now some prompts, to catch what's hiding.

## Phase 2: Work and promises
### Introduction
Run your eye down these and add whatever they shake loose: projects started and not finished, promises made to other people, messages to send or answer, meetings to prepare for, things you're waiting on from someone, money owed or due, deadlines, decisions to make, things to learn.
### Questions
**2.1** What did these shake loose?
### Transition
And the rest of life.

## Phase 3: Home and the rest
### Introduction
The same with these: the home, things to fix or buy, health and appointments, family, friends, birthdays and events coming up, errands, paperwork, bills and accounts, things you've meant to do for fun.
### Questions
**3.1** What did these shake loose?
### Transition
Last, sort the whole list.

## Phase 4: Sort it
### Introduction
Go down everything you've written. For each item, one of four: do it now if it takes two minutes; write the next physical action; park it for someday; or let it go.
### Questions
**4.1** What are the next actions, and what are you letting go?
