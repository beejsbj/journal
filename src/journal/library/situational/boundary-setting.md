## Phase 1: What keeps happening
### Introduction
A boundary is about what you will do, not what someone else must do. Start with the thing you keep putting up with.
### Questions
**1.1** What keeps happening that you're not okay with? Who is involved, and how often does it come up?
**1.2** What does it cost you: time, energy, sleep, how you feel about them or about yourself?
### Transition
Now what you'd like instead.

## Phase 2: What you want
**2.1** What would you like to be true instead? Put it as something you'll do: "When this happens, I will…"
**2.2** What does this limit protect, that matters to you?
### Transition
Then the part that makes it hard.

## Phase 3: What makes it hard
**3.1** What are you afraid will happen if you say it? And what does it cost to keep not saying it?
**3.2** How might they react? How do you want to answer if they push back, sulk or forget?

## Phase 4: The words
**4.1** Write what you'll say in a sentence or two: plain, kind, and without a long explanation.
**4.2** When and where will you say it? And what will you do the next time the line is crossed?
