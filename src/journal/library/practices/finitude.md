- As best you can tell today, what do you think you're here for?
- If your life ended sooner than you expect, what would you regret not having done or said?
- Where are you living on a compromise you've stopped noticing?
- What are you putting off until some later, better time?
- Think of someone who has died. What did they love about ordinary days?
- If something you did today were the last time you did it, which would you want to have noticed?
