- How many days this week did you keep your practice, whatever yours is? How was it when you did, and what got in the way when you didn't?
- Did you do one thing this week only for the pleasure of it, on your own? What was it like?
- Did anything line up unexpectedly this week: a coincidence, a door that opened?
- How did your mood move across the week?
- Is there anything else from this week that mattered and wants saying?
