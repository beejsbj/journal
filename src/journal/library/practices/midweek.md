## 1. Energy Check
- How's your energy been this week?
- High/low/variable? Any idea why?

## 2. Drift Check
- Monday's intentions vs. where you are now — aligned or drifted?
- Anything taking you off course?

## 3. Support Check
- What do you need this week?
- Who can help? What would help?
